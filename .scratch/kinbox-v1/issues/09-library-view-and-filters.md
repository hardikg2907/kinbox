# Library view and filters

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03, 05, 07
Map: [Kinbox v1](../map.md)

## Question

How does browsing and filtering the Library look on a phone and on a desktop?

- Status views; filters: status, genre, length, recommended by, Movie or Series, plus anything the organize decision adds.
- Sort orders, for the Library and for a List (a List shows its Owner's order by default).
- From [Lists, tags and order](03-lists-tags-and-order.md): pinned Entries sit at the top of To watch; the Owner drags to reorder a List.
- From [Statuses and watches](02-statuses-and-watches.md): four Statuses (To watch, Watching, Watched, Dropped); the watched log lists Watches by date, with "date unknown" ones handled sensibly.
- What "length" means for a Series (episode runtime, episodes left, seasons).
- From [Series progress](05-series-progress.md): caught-up Series stay in Watching with a "caught up" marker, so Watching may group them lower; Watching can list the most recently watched Series first; a Watched Series can show a "new season out" marker; the screen words for these markers come from the [Series progress screen](20-series-progress-screen.md).
- Framework-free prototype.
