# Market voice research

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: [Kinbox v1](../map.md)

## Question

Do other people share the pain Kinbox targets, and what do they ask for?

- Pain to test: movies and series split across apps; to-watch lists scattered across streaming apps, screenshots and notes; trouble choosing what to watch tonight; losing your place in a series.
- Sources: Reddit, X, Hacker News, app-store reviews of the competitor apps, forums.
- Output: evidence (quotes + links) for or against the problem statement, and a ranked list of features people wish for, marked v1-relevant or future.

## Answer

Real people share the pains, but not all with the same strength. Evidence comes from X, Hacker News, App Store reviews and forums. Reddit was blocked, so it is not covered.

- **Movies and Series split across apps: strong.** About 15 direct posts, many from 2024-2026. Letterboxd still has no Series. Risk: Letterboxd says it is "still working on it", and apps like Sofa Time and Trakt already cover both.
- **Scattered to-watch lists: some.** Notes-app use and "10 different sites" are real, but the quotes are mostly 2020-2022. No post about screenshots found.
- **Choosing tonight: some.** A few clear user posts. Most are about all of streaming, not about one's own saved list.
- **Losing your place in a Series: some.** Users one-star apps that lose progress (2026). Few say "I lose my place" in 2023-2026.
- **Top wishes:** free with no ads or forced account; Movies and Series in one place; few-tap logging; reliable Series progress; sort and filter the to-watch list. Recommendations, streaming availability, stats, social and import are marked future. No post asks for voice input.
