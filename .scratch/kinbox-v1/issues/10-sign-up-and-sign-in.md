# Sign-up and sign-in

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Map: [Kinbox v1](../map.md)

## Question

How do people sign up and sign in?

- Email link, password, Google / Apple, passkey.
- Can someone try Kinbox before making an account (a guest Library they keep later)? The owner wants this: a Guest who uses Kinbox locally, without an account, and keeps the Library on sign-up. Decide how it works and what a Guest cannot do.
- From [Sharing in v1](01-sharing-in-v1.md): a Guest has a local-only account and can set a display name, but has no Profile and no Share links. A username is chosen only when an Owner first turns the Profile on, not at sign-up. An Owner with an account gives a display name the first time they share or turn the Profile on.
- Usernames or handles, if sharing makes anything public.
- v0.1 uses Better Auth with email + password and closed sign-up ([v0.1 scope](../../../docs/v0.1-scope.md)). This ticket decides public sign-up, Guests and other sign-in methods on top of it.
