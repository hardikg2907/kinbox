# Lists, tags and order

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: [Kinbox v1](../map.md)

## Question

In the core loop save -> organize -> note -> rate, what does "organize" mean in v1?

- Status plus filters only, or also custom lists ("Halloween", "with Dad"), tags, a manual order or "up next" priority, pinning.
- The smallest set that makes organizing feel real and not like a notes app.

## Fixed inputs

- From [Sharing in v1](01-sharing-in-v1.md): Lists exist in v1. Every Title in a List is also in the Library; adding a new Title to a List saves it to the Library as to watch. Lists are what Share links share. Each List is private or public, and public Lists show on the Profile.

## Answer

Organizing in v1 means Statuses, filters, Lists and pins. Nothing else.

- **Lists.** No visible limit on how many Lists an Owner makes or how many Titles a List holds; a high hidden safety limit may exist against abuse. A List has a name and an optional short description, which shows on its shared page. A List keeps the Owner's own order: a new Title goes to the bottom and the Owner can drag to reorder. Viewers can also sort a List while looking at it (sort options belong to Library view and filters). Removing a Title from a List keeps it in the Library.
- **Pins.** An Owner can pin To watch Entries, with no limit. Pinned Entries show at the top of To watch, for the Owner and on the Profile when its To watch switch is on. A pin comes off when the Entry becomes Watched or Dropped. Pins do not affect Lists.
- **Not in v1.** Tags, and a note on each Title in a List. Both are in [future scope](../../../docs/future-scope.md).
