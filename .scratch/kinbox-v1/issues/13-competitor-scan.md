# Competitor scan

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: [Kinbox v1](../map.md)

## Question

What do the apps a movie-and-series fan uses today offer and lack?

- Apps: Letterboxd, Serializd, TV Time, Trakt, Simkl, and the notes-app habit (Google Keep).
- For each: movies, series or both; to-watch list; logging and diary; series progress; ratings and reviews; notes; filters; sharing; import / export; price and paid tier.
- Output: which features are table stakes for Kinbox v1, and which gaps Kinbox can own.

## Answer

- No app leaves the middle empty. Trakt and Simkl already track Movies and Series together, with a to-watch list and episode progress. Both are heavy, and key parts sit behind paid tiers (Trakt filters and rewatch; Simkl rewatch and custom lists).
- Letterboxd, the log style this user likes, still has no returning Series. It has promised them since Jan 2024 and said "still working on it" in Jul 2026. This is a live risk for the pitch.
- TV Time shut down on 2026-07-15. Its users need a new home.
- Table stakes: one catalogue; to-watch list; dated log with free rewatch; episode progress with "mark earlier episodes watched"; half-star rating; private note; basic sort and filter; simple lists or tags; free; phone and desktop web.
- Gaps to own: a "tonight" filter built for deciding; fast phone capture; no caps or paywall on core logging; private by default with no feed; Movies and Series on equal terms.
- Out of scope for v1 (marked in the findings): recommendations, social feeds, stats, streaming availability, import.
