Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What can a person do when the Title they want is not in TMDB, or its entry is thin?

- Options: nothing (search says "not found"); a manual Title that only this person sees; a link that sends them to add it on TMDB.
- CONTEXT.md says Kinbox never creates Titles. A manual Title would change that definition.
- TMDB is edited by its users, so regional titles may be thin.
