## Question

Which framework, database and host fit one developer and a free public app that may make money later?

- Weigh: the owner's skills (strongest in Elixir/Phoenix), a fast mobile-first UI with type-ahead search, calls to and caching of the movie database, the sign-in choice, cost at low traffic.
- May need a short research step on host prices before deciding.
- v0.1 already runs on Next.js (App Router), Postgres on Neon, Drizzle, Better Auth and Vercel Hobby ([v0.1 scope](../../../docs/v0.1-scope.md)). The owner does not want Elixir. This ticket checks that stack against public launch needs (traffic, cost, commercial terms) and changes only what fails.
