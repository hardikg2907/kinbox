# What competitors let you rate on a Series

For: [Series progress](../issues/05-series-progress.md). Gathered 2026-10-11. trakt.tv and several help pages did not load, so many facts are unconfirmed.

| App | Series | Season | Episode | Scale | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Serializd | yes | yes | yes | probably 5 stars | All three confirmed ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/serializd/id1581244120): "Rate individual episodes, seasons, or entire series"). Scale unconfirmed. |
| Trakt | yes | yes | yes | 1-10 | Unconfirmed: seen only through API libraries ([pytrakt](https://pytrakt.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tv.html)). |
| IMDb | yes | no | yes | 1-10 | Confirmed ([help](https://help.imdb.com/article/imdb/track-movies-tv/ratings-faq/G67Y87TFYYP6TWAV)). A series rating is not an average of episode ratings. |
| TV Time (closed) | yes | no evidence | yes | not found | Unconfirmed (Wikipedia). Episodes also had "feelings" and a favourite character. |
| Sofa Time | yes | no evidence | yes | series out of 10 | Episode rating confirmed (App Store note 7.4.0). Copies TV Time's emotions and favourite character. |
| Simkl | yes | no | no | 1-10 | Staff, May 2025: season and episode rating "Not yet, but it is on to-do". |
| Letterboxd | films only | – | – | ½-5 stars | Said Series would come "in a way that doesn't disrupt the current experience". No details. |

## Notes

- A rating for the whole Series is universal.
- An episode rating is common in series-first apps (Serializd, TV Time, Sofa Time, IMDb, probably Trakt).
- A season rating is rarer. Only Serializd confirms it. Trakt probably has it.
- No complaints found about being asked to rate too much in these sources. The TV Time user who left over "review every episode" comes from [market voice](market-voice.md).
- Wishes for season and episode ratings exist (Simkl users, Trakt users in market voice).
