# Kinbox v0.1 decisions

Product decisions made while building v0.1, for questions the [v0.1 scope](v0.1-scope.md) did not answer. The scope is updated to match each one; this file records why.

## 2026-10-16

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What does "Next" show when the Episode after the last watched one is unaired or missing, but an earlier aired Episode is unwatched? | The earliest unwatched aired Episode. "Caught up" shows only when no aired Episode is unwatched. | "Caught up" means every aired Episode is ticked, so a gap must still have a "Next". |
| 2 | Deleting the last Watch when the Status is Dropped or Watching. | The Status changes to To watch only when it was Watched. Otherwise it stays. | The rule exists so a Watched Entry always has a Watch. It must not undo a Dropped or Watching choice. |
| 3 | At save, does the picked Episode mean the next to watch or the last watched? | The next Episode to watch. The Episodes before it are ticked; the picked one is not. The screen says "next to watch". | The card then shows "Next: <picked>", which matches what the Owner just chose. |
| 4 | Episodes with no air date in TMDB. | Aired when any later Episode has aired; unaired otherwise. | Old and regional Series often lack dates for Episodes that aired long ago. Treating all of them as unaired would make "Next" skip them. |
| 5 | Which "today" dates a Watch? | The Owner's time zone, from the phone or browser. | A Watch logged late at night in India must not get the next or previous day from server time (UTC). |
| 6 | When does a Special with no air date count as aired? | When any Episode of the numbered Seasons has aired. | Specials sit outside the season order, so "a later Episode has aired" does not apply. This only decides whether it shows greyed. |
| 7 | Does one-tap Watched on a Series, or picking the next Episode at save, tick Specials? | No. Both tick aired Episodes of the numbered Seasons only. | Specials never count for "Next" or finishing, and ticking dozens of OVAs would be noise. |
| 8 | How should v0.1 look and feel? | A cinematic, calm, dark-first design (light mode supported) with posters as the hero. Gradients and motion are used with purpose: poster scrims, glow on primary actions, tick and "Next" animations, smooth season expand, View Transitions between screens, skeleton loading, optimistic UI on every capture tap. | The owner wants a polished product, not a side project. Motion animates only transform and opacity (about 150-300 ms), respects reduced motion, and keeps WCAG AA contrast, so speed and access do not suffer. |
| 9 | Can a Watching Series be advanced from the Library card? | Yes. The card has the same one-tap "Watched it" as the Entry page; it ticks the current "Next". A short "Undo" follows every tap. A caught-up card has no button. | It serves "losing your place" and one-tap capture with almost no extra code. Undo covers accidental taps while scrolling. |
| 10 | Where does adding a Title start? | Search sits at the top of the Library; typing replaces the grid with results. | Capture is the worst pain, so it starts on the first screen with no extra tap. |

## 2026-10-17

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | How long does the "Watched it" Undo stay? | 8 seconds, the same as the other action toasts. It pauses on hover and on keyboard focus. The tick can always be undone later by unticking the Episode on the Entry page. | 5 seconds was short for a phone. A pause and a second path to undo keep the timed toast within WCAG 2.2.1 (Timing Adjustable). |
| 12 | If the Owner changes the "next to watch" pick right after saving, does the new pick add to the old one? | No. The pick sets the place: aired Episodes of the numbered Seasons before it are ticked, and the ones from it onward are unticked. It only applies in the save flow, where no real progress exists yet. | Otherwise a corrected pick (S3 E1, then S2 E1) leaves Season 2 ticked, and the screen and the Library disagree. |
| 13 | How is an Episode numbered 0 inside a numbered Season handled? | As a normal Episode that comes first in its Season. It can be ticked, unticked and undone. | TMDB lists some pilots as E0. If it cannot be ticked, the Series can never move to Watched by itself. |
| 14 | When does stored Title data refresh if the Owner never opens the Entry page? | Reading the Library also refreshes stale Titles, in the background after the response. | TMDB's terms allow 6 months at most. Refreshing only on the Entry page misses Titles saved straight as Watched. |
| 15 | What happens with a Title that is not out yet (for example a film releasing in December)? | It shows "Out <date>" (a Series: "Premieres <date>") in results, on cards and on the Entry page. Saving offers To watch only. The Entry page's Status menu still allows Watching or Watched. "Tonight" filters leave it out. The label goes away on release day. | Saving an upcoming Title is common; logging it as watched is almost always a mistake. Premieres and festivals still need a way, as unaired Episodes do. |

## 2026-10-18

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | The Library's kind filter (All, Movies, Series) and the "tonight" filters both offer Movie or Series. Where does kind live? | One kind control, inside the filter sheet next to length and genre. There is no separate "Movie or Series" tonight option. Active filters show as removable chips above the grid, and the filter button shows how many are on. | Two controls for one thing confuse. The chips keep a filter that is on visible, so moving kind into a sheet does not hide it. |
| 17 | How does a Title change Status, and with which words? | A Status pill opens an action sheet; press-and-hold on a Library poster opens the same sheet with an "Open" row. The header names the Title and its state ("You're watching this"). Rows are verbs, only valid moves, never the current Status: "Watch later", "Start watching", "Mark as watched" (on Watched: "Watched again"), "Drop it", "Cancel". Helpers state side effects ("Ticks all 62 aired episodes", "Keeps your progress", "Picks up at S2 E4"). Every move shows a toast with Undo that reverses everything the move did. On a Watched Series, "Start watching" keeps the ticks (helper "Keeps your ticks"); clearing them is the rewatch flow, which is not in v0.1. On a Title not out yet, "Start watching" and "Mark as watched" stay, with the helper "Not out yet". The full table is in the design prototypes' STATUS-COPY.md. | A Status change is an action the Owner takes, so it reads as a verb, not a filter. Undo makes every move safe to try. |
| 18 | Screen words for the dock Library, and how press-and-hold behaves | Approved: "Search", "Filters" ("Filters, 1 on"), filter chips with "Remove Movies filter", the filter sheet ("Show": All, Movies, Series; "Layout"; "Clear filters"; "Done"), "Account", "Show more" / "Show less", "Close", and the press-and-hold sheet's "Open" row with the one-time hint "Press and hold a poster for quick actions." Layout is a view setting, not a filter: it never counts in the badge, never becomes a chip, and "Clear filters" leaves it alone; it is remembered on the device, and a phone offers only the layouts that fit (2 columns, 3 columns, List). On desktop, right-click on a poster opens the same sheet, and the hint shows only on touch screens. A quick Status move from the grid must not reach production before every Status move has Undo. | Mixing a view setting with filters makes the count and "Clear" lie. Press-and-hold means nothing with a mouse. A card that leaves the tab with no Undo is the accidental-tap problem that decision 9 solved. |
