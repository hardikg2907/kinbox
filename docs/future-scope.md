# Kinbox future scope

Ideas that are not part of v1. Each one waits for a later effort. Add new ideas here as soon as they come up.

## Next, right after v1 ships

- **Import existing lists.** Bulk paste of titles, then file import from Letterboxd, IMDb and Trakt exports.

## Later

- **Recommendations and discovery**, including a **voice mode**.
- **Social feeds** and following.
- **Stats dashboards.**
- **Streaming availability** (where a Title streams).
- **Collaboration.** Partner Libraries that two people edit, and blends.
- **"Add to my Library" from a shared List.** A Visitor copies a Title into their own Library, with "recommended by" filled in.
- **Profiles that search engines can find**, as a growth channel.
- **Tags**: short labels such as "comfort" or "with Dad", used as filters.
- **A note on each Title in a List**, such as "watch this one first".
- **Partial watch dates** such as "2019" or "March 2019" (v1 allows an exact date or "date unknown").
- **Other episode orders** for a Series, such as an anime's intended or absolute order (v1 uses the movie database's broadcast order only).
- **Season and episode ratings** (v1 rates the whole Series only). Episode ratings are common in series-first apps; season ratings are rarer. See [rating research](../.scratch/kinbox-v1/research/rating-granularity.md).
- **Episode history for each rewatch** (v1 keeps only the Watches of earlier Runs, not their ticks).
- **A Rating on each Watch** (v1 keeps one Rating per Entry: the Owner's current opinion).
- **A "like" or favourite mark** separate from the Rating. Its value is unclear while the Rating covers it.
- **An episode diary**: a date on each watched Episode that the Owner can see and edit.
- **An installable app** (PWA) for the phone home screen.
- **User wishes from market research.** Items marked "future" in the ranked list in [market voice findings](../.scratch/kinbox-v1/research/market-voice.md#d-wished-for-features-ranked).
