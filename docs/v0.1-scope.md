# Kinbox v0.1

v0.1 is the first usable Kinbox: one Owner tracks their own Movies and Series from a phone browser. It ships before the rest of the v1 map is decided. Everything public (Profile, Share links, sign-up) waits for v1.

Terms follow [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md). Glossary terms are for code and planning; screen words must make sense to a user in 2 seconds.

## Stack

| Part | Choice |
|---|---|
| Framework | Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, pnpm |
| UI | Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui. Mobile-first; the desktop is a bigger view of the same screens. |
| Database | Postgres on Neon |
| Database access | Drizzle ORM, migrations with drizzle-kit |
| Sign-in | Better Auth, email + password. Sign-up is closed; a script creates the Owner's account. |
| Hosting | Vercel (Hobby) |
| Movie database | TMDB API v3, called only from the server |

Vercel Hobby and TMDB's free tier are both non-commercial. Once Kinbox earns money, both need paid plans.

## In v0.1

### Sign in
- Email + password sign-in and sign-out. No sign-up page.
- A command creates the Owner's account (for example `pnpm user:create`).
- Every page except sign-in needs a session. Every read and write checks that the Entry belongs to the signed-in Owner.

### Add a Title
- One search box searches Movies and Series together (TMDB multi search), as you type.
- Each result shows the poster, name, year, and Movie or Series.
- Save as **To watch**, **Watching** or **Watched** in one tap.
  - Saving as Watched adds a Watch dated **"date unknown"**, and asks for nothing.
  - Saving a Series as Watching offers an optional pick of the **next Episode to watch** (Season and Episode). It is never required. The Episodes of the numbered Seasons before it are marked watched (not Specials); the picked one is not. The screen words say "next to watch", so nobody reads it as "last watched".
- A Title already in the Library shows its Status instead of the save buttons.
- **Unreleased Titles** (a Movie with a future release date, a Series whose first Episode has not aired; with no date, a TMDB status before "Released") show "Out <date>" or "Premieres <date>" in results, on cards and on the Entry page. Saving offers To watch only. The Entry page's Status menu still allows Watching or Watched, for premieres and festivals. The label goes away on release day.
- An optional Note can be added at save.
- TMDB results that are neither Movie nor Series (people) are not shown.

### Library
- Four tabs: To watch, Watching, Watched, Dropped. A Movie / Series filter.
- Default order: To watch by date added (newest first); Watching by when progress last moved; Watched by the latest Watch date, "date unknown" last; Dropped by date changed.
- Each card shows poster, name, year and kind. Watching Series show "Next: S2 E4" with a one-tap "Watched it" (the same action as on the Entry page), or a "caught up" marker with no button. A short "Undo" follows each tap.
- Search sits at the top of the Library. Typing replaces the grid with results, so capture starts from the first screen.

### Entry page
- Poster, name, year, kind, Status, Watches, Rating, Note, and Series progress for a Series.
- Change the Status. Delete the Entry, after a confirm.

### Statuses and Watches
- Statuses: To watch, Watching, Watched, Dropped. Any Status can move to any other.
- The Status and the Watches always agree:
  - Moving to Watched adds a Watch. Adding a Watch sets Watched.
  - Marking an Entry already in the Library as Watched dates the Watch **today**.
  - Moving away from Watched keeps earlier Watches.
  - Deleting the last Watch of a Watched Entry sets the Status to To watch. On any other Status, the Status stays.
- A Watch has a date, or "date unknown". The date shows small; the Owner taps it to change it.
- "Watched again" adds a Watch dated today; the Status stays Watched.
- "Today" is the date in the Owner's own time zone (from the phone or browser), not the server's.
- The Owner can edit or delete any Watch.

### Series progress
- Each Episode is watched or not. Gaps are possible.
- The Entry leads with one line: "Next: S2 E4" and a one-tap "Watched it". "Next" is the first unwatched aired Episode after the last watched one. If there is none there but an earlier aired Episode is unwatched (a gap), "Next" is the earliest unwatched aired Episode.
- Opening a Season shows its Episode ticks and a "mark whole season" tick.
- Ticking an Episode with unticked Episodes before it ticks only that Episode, then offers "Also mark the N earlier episodes?". One tap says yes; ignoring it says no.
- **Specials** (TMDB season 0) are listed and can be ticked. They never count for "Next" or for finishing.
- **Unaired Episodes** show greyed out with their air date. "Next" and "caught up" ignore them. They can still be ticked.
- An Episode with **no air date** counts as aired when any later Episode of the Series has aired, and as unaired otherwise. A Special with no air date counts as aired when any numbered Episode has aired. Old and regional Series often lack dates for Episodes that aired long ago.
- **Finished** means TMDB says the Series ended or was cancelled. Any other TMDB status means more Episodes may come.
- **Caught up**: every aired Episode is ticked and the Series is not finished. The Entry stays Watching and shows a "caught up" marker.
- Status moves from ticks only go forward:
  - Ticking an Episode of a To watch Entry moves it to Watching.
  - When every Episode of the numbered Seasons of a finished Series is ticked, the Entry moves to Watched and gets a Watch dated today.
  - Unticking never changes the Status or removes a Watch.
  - Ticking an Episode of a Dropped Entry moves it back to Watching.
- One-tap Watched on a Series always works, also on an unfinished Series. It marks all aired Episodes of the numbered Seasons, not Specials.
- Episode ticks have no date on screen. Kinbox records when progress last moved, for the Watching order.
- Episode order is TMDB's broadcast order.

### Rating and Note
- One Rating per Entry, ½ to 5 stars in half-star steps. Only a started Entry (Watching, Watched, Dropped) can be rated.
- After "mark watched", optional stars show inline in the confirmation. Ignoring them changes nothing. Episode ticks never show them.
- One Note per Entry, on any Status. Plain text, line breaks kept, about 1,000 characters at most.
- A Rating and Note stay if the Entry goes back to To watch.

### TMDB terms
- Show the TMDB logo and the notice "This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB." on an About page or footer.
- Posters load from `image.tmdb.org`; they are never copied.
- Stored Title data (name, year, kind, poster path, TMDB status) is refreshed when it is older than 6 months at most. Episode lists are fetched from TMDB with a short cache, not stored.
- Episode ticks keep the TMDB season number and episode number, plus the TMDB episode id.

## Not in v0.1

These stay on the [v1 map](../.scratch/kinbox-v1/map.md) or in [future scope](future-scope.md).

- Guests, public sign-up, email login links, Google sign-in.
- Lists, Pins, sort and filter options beyond the tabs and the Movie / Series filter.
- Profile, Share links, Reviews, Recommended by.
- Series rewatch flow, "new season out" marker, "check your place" message.
- Titles TMDB lacks (v0.1 shows "Not found" with no manual add).
- Paste import comes next; it is specified in [paste import](paste-import.md).
- File import, account deletion, data export.

## Setup the owner does by hand

1. On the home network, themoviedb.org does not open (its address looks blocked by the network's DNS). Set the Mac's DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8, or use mobile data, for sign-up and local development. The live app is not affected.
   Create a TMDB account at themoviedb.org, then Settings → API → create a key for personal, non-commercial use. Copy the **API Read Access Token**.
2. Put it in `.env.local` as `TMDB_API_READ_TOKEN` (never with a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` prefix), and in the Vercel project's environment variables.
3. Create the Vercel project and add Neon Postgres to it (Vercel → Storage). This sets `DATABASE_URL`.
